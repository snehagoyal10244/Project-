The Philosophy

Effortless Volume Sculpted for Living

Engineered with a relaxed architectural cocoon silhouette, this cardigan balances cloud-like warmth with fluid structural grace. Knitted using 12-gauge tension across twin-ply combed yarns, the drape falls naturally away from the body without clinging, finished with extended artisanal cuffs and hand-milled horn buttons.

Gauge & Weight 12-Gauge Double Ply • 440g
Tailoring Detail Seamless Circular Looped Body

Fiber Purity & Composition

  • 100% Grade-A Mongolian Underfleece: Sourced strictly from free-ranging Capra hircus goats in the Alashan region.
  • Average Micron Fineness: 15.2–15.5 microns with 36mm staple length ensuring anti-pilling longevity.
  • Fastenings: Ethically gathered buffalo horn buttons, individually turned and hand-buffed in Tuscany.

Mindful Herding & Regenerative Grasslands

NOVARA partners exclusively with nomadic Mongolian cooperatives practicing rotational grazing to halt desertification. Each goat is hand-combed during springtime molt—never sheared—protecting animal welfare and producing cleaner, longer staple fibers.
